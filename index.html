Authorized Security Audit – Academic Project

As part of a group cybersecurity course project, I participated in an authorized security assessment for a small local organization. The project focused on identifying potential security risks through reconnaissance and high-level analysis.

My primary responsibility involved reconnaissance activities, including gathering publicly available information to understand the organization’s digital footprint and identify potential exposure points. Findings were documented and discussed as part of a collaborative team report, along with recommendations for improving security posture.

This project was conducted in an academic setting with explicit authorization and did not involve exploitation or unauthorized access.
